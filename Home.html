About Real Madrid C.F.

"Founded on March 6, 1902, as Madrid Football Club, Real Madrid Club de Fútbol is globally recognized as one of the most prestigious and successful sports institutions in history. Officially recognized by FIFA as the Best Club of the 20th Century, the Spanish giant is synonymous with football excellence, sporting ambition, and a relentless winning culture.:

Heritage & Key Details

  • Nicknames: Commonly referred to as Los Blancos (The Whites) and Los Merengues due to their iconic all-white home kit.
  • Domestic Dominance: Real Madrid holds the record for the most La Liga titles in Spanish football history.
  • European Royalty: The club stands as the undisputed ruler of European football, holding the record for the most UEFA Champions League trophies won.
  • Home Ground: The world-famous Santiago Bernabéu Stadium, located in the heart of Madrid, Spain. The arena features state-of-the-art retractable pitch and roof technology for year-round sports and events.
" "

Real Madrid operates as a member-owned sports club (socios), meaning thousands of registered members collectively own and vote on club governance. Over the decades, the team has been defined by hosting many of football's greatest global icons alongside talent developed at its world-renowned youth academy, La Fábrica, consistently competing for top domestic and international honors.
